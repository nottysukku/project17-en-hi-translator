Project 17 — STM32N6570-DK + NUCLEO-WBA55CG → TMUX1574 → MAX98357A → 8 Ω 0.5 W speaker

Two I2S masters share one speaker. Side A is the WBA's Bluetooth LE Audio music: the default, and the only source while the N6 sleeps. Side B is the N6's English-to-Hindi translator. The N6 flips SEL, mutes the amplifier during each switch, and listens to the WBA's "music playing" line. No battery: the amplifier runs from the DK's 5 V pin, and each board from its own USB cable.

Wire it in this order

Numbers match the red markers on the drawing. Keep both USB cables unplugged while wiring, and check the grounds, the 5 V line and the GAIN resistor before you power up.

1+3V3 → TMUX VDD

DK CN8 pin 4 → TMUX pin 16. Solder a 100 nF ceramic from pin 16 to pin 8, right at the chip.

2Common ground

Join DK CN8 pin 6, TMUX pin 8, MAX GND and Nucleo CN5 pin 6. Also tie TMUX pins 12–15 to ground (EN low = always on; channel 4 unused). Every ⏚ is this one net.

3DK +5V → MAX VIN

DK CN8 pin 5 (the board's 5 V output) → MAX VIN. No battery or separate supply: the speaker adds at most about 0.1 A. A 100 µF capacitor across VIN and GND at the module is optional.

4A2 = PE12 → S1B

N6 bit clock, SAI2_SCK_B, AF10. DK CN7 pin 3 → TMUX pin 3. 48 kHz × 32 bits = 1.536 MHz.

5D6 = PE13 → S2B

N6 word select, SAI2_FS_B. DK CN11 pin 7 → TMUX pin 6. Low = left slot, high = right slot.

6D8 = PE7 → S3B

N6 data, SAI2_SD_B, I2S Philips, 16-bit. DK CN12 pin 1 → TMUX pin 10. Both slots carry the same mono sample.

7PB6 → S1A (dashed)

WBA bit clock, SAI1_SCK_B. Nucleo CN8 pin 4 ("D3") → TMUX pin 2. PB6 is also user button B2.

8PB5 → S2A (dashed)

WBA word select, SAI1_FS_B. Nucleo morpho CN4 pin 35 → TMUX pin 5. Arduino D1 would need a solder bridge, so use the morpho pin.

9PB7 → S3A (dashed)

WBA data, SAI1_SD_B. Nucleo CN8 pin 3 ("D2") → TMUX pin 11. PB7 is also user button B3. ST's own LE Audio example uses these three pins.

10TMUX → MAX I2S

TMUX pin 4 → BCLK, pin 7 → LRC, pin 9 → DIN. Keep these three short. Go by the names on your module's silkscreen; its pin order may differ from the drawing.

11D7 = PD6 → SEL

DK CN11 pin 8 → TMUX pin 1, with 100 kΩ to ground. Low = side A = WBA music, which is also the state while the N6 sleeps. High = side B = N6.

12D4 = PH5 → 1 kΩ → SD

DK CN11 pin 5, open-drain. Pulled low, the amplifier is off for about 40 ms around each switch. Released, the module's own bias gives the (L+R)/2 mix. Measure SD first: 0.4–0.5 V is right; about 0 V means add 1 MΩ from VIN to SD.

13PB0 → D2 = PD0

Nucleo CN8 pin 7 ("D6") → DK CN11 pin 3, with 100 kΩ to ground at the DK. The WBA holds it high while music plays; the N6 hands over the speaker and sleeps.

14GAIN → 100 kΩ → VIN

Required for the 0.5 W speaker. It sets 3 dB: a full-scale sine is 1.8 Vrms, 0.40 W into 8 Ω. Left open (9 dB), the same sine is 1.6 W, three times the speaker's rating. The N6 firmware also lowers its own output by 3 dB.

15Speaker 8 Ω · 0.5 W

MAX OUT+ / OUT− screw terminal → speaker. The output is bridge-tied: never connect either speaker wire to ground, to the USB shield, or to another amplifier.

How the switch decides

AUTO mode on the N6

  • Music line high for 0.1 s: amp muted, SEL low, amp back on. The N6 pauses recognition and its own I2S, and the screen goes dark after 5 s.
  • Music line low for 1.5 s: the N6 wakes, SEL goes high, and two short beeps confirm the speaker path.
  • A tap or the USER button lights the screen for 10 s. TRANSLATE and MUSIC force one side. TONE plays 880 Hz.

VIN6 dB2.5 Vrms = 0.81 W: too much
open9 dB3.6 Vrms ≈ 1.6 W: too much

Full scale is 2.1 dBV plus the gain (MAX98357A data sheet). The N6 adds a −3 dB cap (XL_AUDIO_OUT_CEILING_Q15), so its speech and beeps stay well under 0.5 W. The WBA music gets the same cap when its firmware is ported. Until then, the GAIN resistor is what protects the speaker.

Safety and power

  • The MAX98357A drives both speaker terminals with fast PWM. Shorting either one to ground can destroy it.
  • Power the DK by USB-C on CN6 from a USB-C port or charger. UM3300: the board can then draw 1.66 or 3.2 A. An old USB-A port can limit it to 0.55 A.
  • The Nucleo runs from its own ST-LINK USB cable. Its ground still joins the common ground.
  • The DK and Nucleo pins are 3.3 V. Never put 5 V on a header signal pin.

Pins avoided, and why

  • PD12 on the DK is the Ethernet chip's MDIO line, so SEL is on D7 = PD6.
  • PA8 on the Nucleo is the ST-LINK virtual COM port's RX line, so the WBA uses SAI1 block B instead of block A.
  • SD tied straight to 3.3 V selects the left channel only, and music would lose its right channel. SD is biased for the (L+R)/2 mix instead.
  • Stereo later: a second MAX98357A on the same BCLK, LRC and DIN lines, one set to left (SD above 1.4 V) and one to right (0.77–1.4 V). Each needs its own 8 Ω speaker and GAIN resistor. Both can run from the DK's 5 V.

Signal chain: N6 SAI2_B (A2 / D6 / D8) and WBA SAI1_B (PB6 / PB5 / PB7) → TMUX1574 (SEL = D7) → MAX98357A (SD = D4, GAIN 3 dB) → 8 Ω 0.5 W speaker. Music line: Nucleo PB0 → DK PD0. Power: DK USB-C → CN8 pin 5 (+5 V) → amplifier; Nucleo on its own USB.
